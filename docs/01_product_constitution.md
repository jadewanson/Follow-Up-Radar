# Product Constitution v1

## Product
Follow-Up Radar is a narrow control layer for service-based freelancers and micro-studios that prevents invoice, proposal, lead, and promised-reply follow-ups from being lost until terminal outcome.

## ICP
- solo freelancers
- micro-studios (1–3 people)
- service businesses
- browser/email/chat/document heavy workflows
- dislike heavy CRMs

## Hero use case
A user sends an invoice, proposal, or promised reply, receives no response, saves a follow-up, gets reminded at the correct time, returns, acts, and closes the case as paid/done/lost.

## Core loop
Capture → Schedule → Remind → Act → Close

## Activation
A user creates at least one follow-up item, receives a reminder, returns from that reminder, and closes or reschedules the case.

## Non-goals
- CRM
- inbox replacement
- generic task management
- accounting suite
- AI assistant
- collaboration workspace
- mobile-first product
