# Iteration 12 — Sale Layer / Beta Readiness

## Goal
Add a minimal sale layer and beta-readiness surface without pretending a full billing suite exists.

## Included
- public site config endpoint
- public pricing, support, privacy, terms, and onboarding pages
- authenticated billing page
- beta interest capture endpoint and storage

## Notes
This iteration intentionally avoids fake checkout or fake customer portals. The product gets the surfaces required to present plans, collect upgrade intent, and run a controlled beta.
